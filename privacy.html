Privacy Policy

Niggun Uploader · Last updated November 8, 2026

Niggun Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Niggunim with the Rebbe YouTube channel to upload recordings to that channel. This policy explains what it does with Google account data.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in, the app asks Google for permission to manage that YouTube account. It uses this permission only to:

  • upload videos to the channel;
  • set each video's title, description and thumbnail;
  • create playlists on the channel and add videos to them.

It does not read, collect or store viewers' information, comments, subscribers, email, contacts or any other personal data.

What is stored

The app keeps a sign-in token so it can upload without asking again, plus a list of the videos and playlists it has created. These are stored privately by the channel owner and are not shared with anyone.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties. Videos are published to YouTube, where Google's own privacy policy applies.

Google API Services

Niggun Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
