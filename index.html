Niggun Uploader

ניגונים שנוגנו אצל הרבי

Niggun Uploader is a small private tool that publishes recordings of niggunim sung at farbrengens with the Lubavitcher Rebbe to the Niggunim with the Rebbe YouTube channel.

Once a day it takes the next few niggunim from our archive, cuts each recording to the start and end of the niggun, and uploads it with its name, date and farbrengen. Each video is added to a playlist for that niggun and a playlist for that farbrengen. It does not run on Shabbos or Yom Tov.

The tool only acts on our own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
